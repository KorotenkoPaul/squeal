
Title: First Impressions
Author: Rich Samuels
Description: Ellen invites her new boyfriend over for dinner with her family. First impressions mean everything, so Jim’s a bit nervous. Ellen isn’t worried, though. Her family is convinced that she has good taste in men.
Ellen
I would really like you over for dinner.
Jim
Okay!
Ellen
To my parent’s place.
Jim
I know. I knew it was coming.
Ellen 
I think they would like you.
Jim
I have to admit, I’m a little nervous.
Ellen
You don’t need to be. Everyone will like you.
Jim
Everyone?
Ellen
My brothers and sisters.
Jim
Everyone’s going to be there.
Ellen
Of course! Everyone wants to be a part of it.
Jim
Wow, your family really likes being part of your life.
Ellen
Yeah, they’re like that.
Jim
What’s your mom like?
Ellen
She’s be fine. She’ll like you.
Ellen
She’ll be happy you have “meat on your ones” lol
Jim
I’m still trying to lose it.
Ellen
But you won’t by next weekend, so that’s okay.
Jim
Yeah. 
Ellen
She’s going to be so happy I found someone I would like.
Jim
Would like?
Ellen
Well, DO like.
Jim
How many brothers and sisters do you have?
Ellen
Five, I’m the oldest.
Jim
Well, that’s good at least.
Ellen
I guess. It’s a responsibility.
Ellen
You know, sometimes I have to take care of them.
Ellen
Make sure they’re fed.
Jim
Well, at least you’re older now. You can have your own life.
Ellen
That’s true.
Jim
Your family sounds nice, anyway.
Ellen
They are nice.
Ellen
Like I said, everyone’s going to like you.
Ellen
They’ve got good taste.
Jim
Well, I don’t know about that!
Ellen
You have to trust me on this.
Jim
Are they going to be, like, protective?
Ellen
Well, no.
Ellen
They know how to share.
Jim
That’s good.
Jim
I’ll do my best to make a good impression.
Ellen
Like they say you only get one chance to make a first impression.
Jim
I know. They’ll like me, I promise.
Ellen
Oh, one thing.
Jim
What’s that?
Ellen
Don’t wear cologne.
Jim
Are they allergic?
Ellen
Sort of.
Jim
Ok. No problem.
------
Friday Afternoon
Jim
Ellen?
Ellen
Hi, you all ready?
Jim
I’ll be heading over in a little while.
Ellen
Great! They’re all looking forward to you.
Ellen
….to seeing you.
Jim
I’m looking forward to seeing them, too!
Jim
So, what we having for dinner?
Ellen
Steak probably.
Ellen
My mom loves steak. So does my dad.
Ellen
Well, everyone.
Ellen
We’re all steak eaters.
Jim
I guess it’s steak, then!
Jim
Good thing I’m not a vegetarian!
Ellen
Good thing we’re not!
Jim
I bet it’s going to be great.
Ellen
It is, my mom’s a great cook.
Jim
So, should I pick you up at 6:30?
Ellen
No, you could just go straight to their house.
Ellen
I’m helping my parents get ready.
Ellen
There’s a lot of work to do.
Jim
Yeah, I guess there would be.
Ellen
They’re going to barbeque.
Jim
Awesome! I love barbeque.
Ellen
You can actually come a little earlier, if you want.
Jim
How early?
Ellen
I’ll be there at four. You could come then. 
Ellen
Then they wouldn’t have to rush.
Jim
Okay. I can do that.
Ellen
Great! I’ll tell my mom. She’ll be happy!
Ellen
So will by brothers and sisters!
Ellen
They like to eat early.
Jim
I bet it takes time to cook that much steak.
Ellen
It’s getting it ready that takes the time.
Ellen
Before you cook it.
Jim
Like marinating it or something?
Ellen
Just, the butchering. You know, carving it up…
Jim
Wow, you make it sound like you have a whole cow there!
Ellen
Hardly. I promise you, we have NO cow.
Jim
Well that’s a relief!
Jim
This sounds like it’s going to be an awesome dinner.
Ellen
It will!
Ellen
It’s going to be delicious.
Jim
Ellen, I’m really happy to be part of your life.
Jim
And maybe even part of your family.
Ellen
So are we.
Ellen
My mom says I have good taste in men.
