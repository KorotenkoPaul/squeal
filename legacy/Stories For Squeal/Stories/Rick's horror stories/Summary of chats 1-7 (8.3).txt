
HORROR CHAT STORIES
1-7

Title: First Impressions
Description: Ellen invites her new boyfriend over for dinner with her family. First impressions mean everything, so Jim’s a bit nervous. Ellen isn’t worried, though. Her family is convinced that she has good taste in men.
Name: Haunted
Description: For two hundred dollars, Carl and David decide to take up the challenge and spent the night in a haunted house. After all, haunted houses aren’t real—and they have nothing to worry about…do they?
Name: Skinless
Description: Joe discovers that he can peel a scab off of his arm without any pain. Naturally, he continues peeling and peeling…and peeling…just to see what happens. Things get a little sticky.
Name: The Doctor
Description: Josh, camping alone and miles from civilization, is wounded and needs medical help badly. Unable to hike to safety, his friend arranges for a doctor to offer some advice via texting.  
Name: Nobody’s Home Part 1
Description: When Patrick finally arrives at Sandra’s party, her house is completely dark and no one’s home. At first, Patrick is convinced he’s the victim of a prank, but his friend James has other ideas. 
Name: Nobody’s Home Part 2
Description: Terrified, Patrick returns to his own home, and finds that the house dark and the street deserted. James comes to help, but though they’re both standing in front of the house, they can’t see each other.
Name: Nobody’s Home Part 3
Description: Patrick insists that he isn’t dead, despite all of the evidence, and sets off back to Sandra’s house to find everyone and reveal the prank once and for all. He finds something else, entirely.
