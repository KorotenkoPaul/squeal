Title: Perfect Match

Author: Asjad Ullah

Description: Sam thinks he does not love his wife, he starts searching for a mate to spice things up a little, using a fake ID on a dating website. At last he finds a perfect match, but it turns out, the match is just too perfect for him to continue.
