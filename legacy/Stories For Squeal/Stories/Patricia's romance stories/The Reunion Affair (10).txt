Title: The Reunion Affair

Author: Patricia Johnson

Description: Mary and Bill were high school sweethearts but the war separated them. They married another but at the reunion they found the sparks were still there.

Bill: Is this a bad time to text Mary?

Mary: No it is not Bill. Jack is gone to work.

Bill: It was sure nice to see you at the reunion.

Mary: It was. You have not changed a bit.

Bill: Then you must need glasses.

Mary: To me you are still the handsome man I loved.

Bill: And you are still the sexy cheerleader.

Mary: Now who needs glasses?

Bill: Will you meet me tonight?

Mary: I am married Bill.

Bill: So am I but the spark is still there.

Mary: Yes it is but we are both married.

Bill: I know that. You have reminded me enough.

Mary: We should not have kissed.

Bill: Yes we should have. It was right.

Mary: I have never cheated on Jack.

Bill: And I have never cheated on Sue.

Mary: Then why are we talking about it?

Bill: Because we still desire each other.

Mary: This may be true but does not make it right.

Bill: The war separated us.

Mary: We should have married before you shipped out.

Bill: We didn’t but you should have waited. You promised.

Mary: They said you had died. I grieved for a year.

Bill: You should have waited longer.

Mary: I know but I was lonely.

Bill: I regret conforming to the rules of that time.

Mary: Me too. But I was afraid of getting pregnant

Bill: I would have married you.

Mary: I know but did not want a “have too marriage.”

Bill: That is the past. This is now. Meet me.

Mary: I have never been with another man.

Bill: I wish I could say the same but cannot.

Mary: I don’t know Bill.

Bill: Don’t turn me down again like you did prom night.

Mary: Okay I will meet you.

Bill: You will? That makes me happy.

Mary: At noon, Airport Motel.

Bill: Don’t chicken out again.

Mary: I won’t.

Bill: I love you. I have protection.

Mary: I love you. What room?

Bill: Room 13.

Mary: Leaving now.

Bill: See you soon my darling.

Mary: Bill, car will not start. I’m going to be late.

Bill: Sorry, but I have to make my flight.

Mary: Please wait.

Bill: I can’t wait. I have to go.

Mary: No\!

Bill: We were never meant to consummate our love.

Mary: I love you Bill forever.

Bill: I will always love you Mary.

Mary: It is so unfair.

Bill: Yes it is but life is not fair.

Mary: Wait, I want to go with you.

Bill: You can’t Mary. It is not your time.

Mary: Not my time. What do you mean?

Bill: Look on your pillow.

Mary: Your dog tags? But this means…

Bill: Yes, I am dead.

Mary: But your body…

Bill: On the Beaches of Normandy…mass grave.

Mary: But this feels so real…the reunion.

Bill: My spirit…saying I love you and good bye my love.
