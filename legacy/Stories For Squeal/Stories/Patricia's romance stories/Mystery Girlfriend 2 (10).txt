
Title: Mystery Girlfriend
Author: Patricia Johnson
Description: Bruce, the son of a billionaire has met the girl of his dreams on Facebook – or so he thinks.  His father is very against his plans to marry her but why?

Genry:  Hey buddy,  why ain’t you answering your texts?
Bruce:  Sorry.  Been busy.
Genry:  Doing what or who?
Bruce:  Met the girl of my dreams on Facebook.
Genry:  Really?  That is what you thought about Linda.
Bruce:  But this is different.
Genry:  Different how?
Bruce:  She understands, is beautiful, sweet, and kind.
Genry:  How long have you known her?
Bruce:  Just two weeks and I love her.
Genry:  After two weeks?
Bruce:  Just she means so much to me.  Love at first sight.
Genry: Sorry, don’t believe in love at first sight or any sight.
Bruce:  Not all girls are bitches are like Colleen.
Genry:  Haven’t found one yet who isn’t.
Bruce:  Do you want me to see if Laila has a sister or friend?
Genry:  No thanks.  I am done with the dating scene for awhile.
Bruce:  If you are sure.
Genry:  I am sure but thanks.
Bruce:  Change your mind let me know.
Genry:  So tell me more about Laila.
Bruce:  She is beautiful with dark hair and eyes and very curvy.
Genry:  What have you two been doing?
Bruce:  I have taken her to romantic beach destinations.
Genry:  Sounds nice.
Bruce:  I have even rented a concert hall just for the two of us.
Genry:  That sounds cool.  
Bruce:  This is real love and is going to last forever.
Genry:  Forever is a long time.
Bruce:  I know that.
Genry:  What is her last name?
Bruce:  Lafontaine.
Genry:  One of the shoe tycoon’s daughters?
Bruce:  No, just a regular girl.
Genry:  How is your father taking it?
Bruce:  Not very well.  He is against it.
Genry:  Yes, your father wanted you to marry Gloria Van House.
Bruce:  Yes, but she is an air head and…
Genry:  And what buddy?
Bruce:  A cold fish in bed.
Genry:  What kind of work does Laila do?
Bruce:  I am not sure.  She works various hours.  
Genry:  She did not tell you?
Bruce:  No and all I know is it is public relations.
Genry:  When do I get to meet her?
Bruce:  I will have to see when she is off work.
Genry:  She has to have my stamp of approval first.
Bruce:  Your approval is all I need.
Genry:  What about your father’s approval?
Bruce:  Father said he would disinherit me if I saw her again.
Genry:  What are you going to do?
Bruce:  Keep seeing her.
Genry:  Even if you lose all your money, cars, house?
Bruce:  Yes because I love her.
Genry:  How is love going to put a roof over your head?
Bruce:  We will make it work.  She has a job.
Genry:  Has your father met her?
Bruce:  No.
Genry:  Then how did your father find out you were seeing someone other than Gloria?
Bruce:  The credit card bills and the money I have been spending on her.
Genry:  A lot of money?
Bruce:  Yes.
Genry:  Wow!  Now what?
Bruce:  Father is taking me to lunch.
Genry:  Where?
Bruce:  The Italian Bistro.  Father insisted this restaurant.
Genry:  Why lunch?
Bruce:  Why not?  You have to eat and that place is good as any.
Genry:  Why that restaurant?  You are not fond of Italian.
Bruce:  Hold on.  Text from Laila.
30 minutes later
Bruce:  I am back.  Father said he is willing to compromise in regards to Laila and me.
Genry:  That is a step in the right direction
 Bruce:  He wants to discuss our upcoming marriage.
Genry:  Enjoy.  Text me to let me know how it went.
The next day
Genry:  How did lunch go?
Bruce:  I got it now why Father invited me to The Italian Bistro.
Genry:  Why?
Bruce:  We were driving to the restaurant.
Genry:  And?
Bruce:  We passed that place where the prostitutes hang out looking for noon time customers.
Genry:  So?  They are there all the time.
Bruce:  Laila was among them.
