Title: Under wrong hands

Author: Rutaba Shahzad

Description: Amy is wrongly accused of the murder of her husband. In between the desperation of proving her innocence and determining the identity of the killer, she finds herself making the wrong choices; unconsciously at first, but deliberately later onwards.

Amy: Winona, are you there?

Winona: Yes, I am. Where are you?

Amy: I can’t say that\! You must have heard.

Winona: Yes, I did. I am so terribly sorry for the death of your husband.

Winona: Why aren’t you here?

Amy: Okay, I know what this looks like but I did not kill him.

Winona: Come on Amy\! You are on the run; you are even using a burner phone.

Winona: You were there on the night of the murder, sleeping beside him.

Amy: How do you know that?

Winona: Well, I saw you guys come home so there is my testimony.

Amy: And that of many other people.

Winona: I am not doing anything illegal.

Amy: Hey, I am not asking you to do anything like that.

Winona: It is a crime to omit or alter testimony, I hope you know that.
