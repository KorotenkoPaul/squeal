
HORROR CHAT STORIES
8-14
Name: The Grave
Description: Aaron receives frantic texts from Jenna, a girl he doesn’t know, and who is claiming to be held captive, trapped in a buried coffin. She begs Aaron for help.

Name:  Great Hammers
Description: Arnold and Kevin discuss and debate their upcoming outing as serial killers, and the effects of pounding various victims on the head with their heavy hammers.
Name: Kitchen
Description: Felix doesn’t want to contribute to helping out in his shared kitchen, but his friends insist. He tries to explain a little problem he has…with knives.
Name:  The Ones Who Watch
Description: Eric is scared of the dark, and never shuts off the lights. Don ridicules Eric’s fear, and blames it all on his imagination…until he discovers the truth.
Name: On the Bus
Description: One day, on his daily commute, Stan realizes that everyone on the bus is staring at him. Not only that, they’re all holding a very large knife. all holding a knife.
Name: Clown
Description: Michael really hates clowns, but now he’s being texted by a very angry Zippo the Clown. Zippo only wants to be friends, but it’s not going well at all.
Name: Slice
Description: A serial killer texts Colin and informs him that he will be the next victim. Colin isn’t convinced at first, but the killer seems to know a little too much about him…and where he is.
